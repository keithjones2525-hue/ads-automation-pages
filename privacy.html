Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Overview

Ads Automation is a private analytics tool for authorized advertising accounts.

Information We Access

With authorization, the tool accesses advertising performance data, including impressions, clicks, advertising costs, and conversion metrics.

How We Use Data

Data is used for advertising performance analysis, reporting, and monitoring. The tool does not modify advertising campaigns, budgets, or account settings.

Data Storage and Security

Retrieved data is stored in a locally managed database. Access is restricted to authorized users. API credentials are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

Advertising data is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties. Reports may be shared with the relevant authorized clients.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as necessary for reporting and analysis. Authorized clients may request deletion of their data.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the application administrator using the support email address provided during authorization.
